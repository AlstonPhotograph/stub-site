Support

Start with the questions below. If you cannot find an answer, submit feedback or email us. Chinese labels are included to help you find the controls in the app.

Where is my ledger stored?

Your ledger is stored on your device by default and is not automatically uploaded to us. Stub does not require an account. Feedback you choose to submit is sent to our server; email feedback is handled by our email provider. Device backups may include app data, and you choose where exported or shared files go. Deleting the app removes its local ledger, so export a backup first.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

In Settings › Data › Backup and Restore (设置 › 数据 › 备份与恢复), export a JSON backup to Files or storage you trust. On your new phone, install Stub and choose Import JSON Backup (导入 JSON 备份) in the same section to restore your books. Backups are not encrypted; keep them somewhere safe.

Can I import from another bookkeeping app?

You can import CSV files. Follow the format shown in Settings › Data › CSV Import and Export (设置 › 数据 › CSV 导入导出). You can preview the import before confirming the batch and undo the batch afterward.

What is free, and what does Pro add?

Bookkeeping, categories, tags, entry templates, category budgets, refunds and reimbursements, loans, credit-card repayments, monthly total budgets, export and backup, App Lock and the Quick Entry widget are free. The free version allows 5 asset accounts and 5 recurring plans. Pro removes those two limits and adds budget rollover, older-month statistics and a 6-month trend, actual cash flow, multiple currencies and books, combined reimbursement receipts, batch management, and budget, spending and plan widgets.

How much does a subscription cost? Is there a trial?

Prices in mainland China are ¥6 monthly, ¥15 for 3 months and ¥36 yearly; all plans provide the same benefits. Prices elsewhere are those shown in the App Store. Eligible first-time yearly subscribers can receive a 7-day free trial, subject to Apple's eligibility check. Cancel before the trial ends to avoid a charge.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name › Subscriptions › Stub, then choose Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends to avoid renewal.

How do I restore purchases after reinstalling or changing phones?

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then open Settings › About › Stub Membership (设置 › 关于 › Stub 会员) and tap Restore Purchases (恢复购买).

What happens to my data when Pro expires?

No data is deleted. Existing entries remain available to view, edit, export and back up. Existing accounts and recurring plans above the free limits remain usable; you cannot add new ones above those limits.

How do I request a refund?

Apple handles subscription payments. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I report a problem or suggest a feature?

There are two paths. In Settings › About › Feedback (设置 › 关于 › 意见反馈), choose Report a Problem or Suggestion. Read the notice about sending personal information outside mainland China, then tap Agree and Continue (同意并继续). If Mail can send, the system composer opens and you can change the message before deciding whether to send it. If not, the app hands it to the default mail app, or asks you to copy the address and feedback ID and write yourself. Alternatively, shake your iPhone to open the in-app feedback form. Tap Send (发送), then Agree and Submit (同意并提交), to send it to our server in mainland China. Canceling sends nothing and does not affect local bookkeeping. You can turn shaking off on the Feedback page.

Feedback includes the app version, system version, model code and a random feedback ID. The email composer shows those prefilled lines so you can edit them before sending. The shake form shows the text you write, optional contact details and the screenshot; the confirmation notice lists the other categories. A problem report includes no-ledger diagnostics, and direct submission has no switch to remove them. Shake feedback can include a screenshot. The phone attempts to cover digits first and does not guarantee that every digit is covered. If that processing fails, no screenshot is attached. Digits, account names, category names and notes may remain visible, so preview the screenshot and decide whether to keep it. Leave contact details if you want a reply. You can also email developer@txturefty.com directly.

How long is feedback kept? What happens offline?

Direct in-app submissions are deleted automatically by our server 365 days after receipt. After a network failure, only feedback you have confirmed for submission is kept locally and retried when you are online. The queue keeps at most 5 items and drops an older unsent item first; anything still unsent after 7 days is deleted. Email feedback may be stored by Google Workspace outside mainland China. We keep it for at most 12 months from the day we receive it, counted as 365 days, the same maximum as a direct submission. We delete these messages manually; the email service does not purge them automatically. You can email us with your feedback ID to request access, correction or deletion; after verification, we will process it within 15 working days. See the Privacy Policy.

Are there rewards for feedback?

Yes. When we adopt a feature suggestion or confirm a reported problem, we reply with a Stub Pro offer code. We decide which suggestions to adopt; for the same issue or suggestion, the first person to report it receives the code. Leave contact details in your in-app submission or email us so we can send it to you. This is unrelated to App Store ratings and reviews.

What is a feedback ID?

It is a random string generated on your phone, such as S-7K2M-QX9D, that helps us match feedback from the same device and reply or send a thank-you code. It does not come from a device identifier, Apple Account or ledger data. Reinstalling the app generates a new one.

How do I redeem an offer code?

Open Settings › About › Stub Membership (设置 › 关于 › Stub 会员), tap Offer Code (兑换码) and follow the confirmation steps. You can also tap your profile photo in the App Store and choose Redeem Gift Card or Code. Apple handles redemption, and membership becomes active after successful redemption. Codes expire, so use them promptly.

What if I cannot unlock the app?

App Lock uses your iPhone's Face ID, Touch ID or device passcode. Use the same authentication you use to unlock your phone.
