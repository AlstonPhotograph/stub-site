Privacy Policy

Stub is a bookkeeping app that stores your ledger locally on your iPhone. There is no account, ledger upload or bank connection. The membership page retrieves public campaign information over the internet; when you choose to email us, we receive your email address and message.

Operator: Texture Factory (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd. · Effective: October 9, 2026 · Updated: October 26, 2026

Data we collect

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Everything you enter in your ledger stays on your device, and we cannot see or access it. Feedback emails are described below.

Public campaign information: The membership page retrieves current Pro campaigns and claim periods over HTTPS from the public campaign configuration on stub.ink. This request does not send ledger data, Apple Account information, feedback IDs or unique device identifiers. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or tracking.

GitHub Pages hosts the campaign configuration and this website. To serve the pages and configuration, the hosting provider GitHub may receive basic network information such as your IP address, request time and requested path. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data stored on your device

Entries, accounts, categories, notes, tags, budgets, recurring plans, templates, refunds and reimbursements, loans, credit-card statements, edit history and app preferences are stored in the app's on-device storage and used only to show your ledger.

Permissions

Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode is used only if you turn on App Lock; iOS performs the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded. Notifications are requested only if you turn on local reminders. The app does not use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendars or health data.

Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app checks your subscription status on-device through StoreKit; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Export and backups

JSON and CSV exports are not encrypted; you decide where they go. Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data and are handled by Apple or your computer. Diagnostics are created only when you export them or attach them to a problem report, and contain no amounts, names, notes or device identifiers.

Feedback and offer codes

Settings › About › Feedback (设置 › 关于 › 意见反馈) opens your mail app with our address, a subject and a few prefilled lines: a feedback ID (a random string such as S-7K2M-QX9D generated on your device, not derived from any device identifier, Apple Account or ledger data, and replaced if you reinstall the app), the app version and build, iOS version and model code (for example iPhone16,1). Problem reports also attach the no-ledger diagnostics. You can see and edit all of it before sending; the feedback email and its attachments are not sent to us unless you tap Send.

When you email us we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. We use it only to reply, fix problems, improve the app, and to send you a Pro offer code as thanks when we adopt your suggestion or confirm a problem you reported. We never use it for advertising, sell it or share it, except where required by law. Mail is stored with our email provider (Google Workspace), whose servers may be outside mainland China, and kept for up to 2 years after the issue is closed; you can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Offer codes are Apple subscription offer codes, redeemed in the app (Stub 会员 › 兑换码) or in the App Store. Apple handles redemption and the subscription; we do not receive your Apple Account details.

Retention and deletion

Ledger data stays on your device until you delete entries, use "Clear all ledger data" in Settings, or delete the app. Files you exported and system backups are not affected. Because we never hold your ledger data, we cannot restore it for you. To access, correct or delete emails you sent us, contact us.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14.

Changes and contact

We will post any update on this page. If we add further online or syncing features, or new features that process personal data, we will explain them before they are enabled and obtain consent where required by law. Contact: developer@txturefty.com.
