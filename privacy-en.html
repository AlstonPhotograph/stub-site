Privacy Policy

Stub is a bookkeeping app that stores your ledger locally on your iPhone. There is no account, ledger upload or bank connection. The membership page retrieves public campaign information over the internet; we receive information from you only when you choose to submit feedback or email us.

Operator: Texture Factory (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd. · Effective: October 9, 2026 · Updated: November 12, 2026

Data we collect

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Everything you enter in your ledger stays on your device, and we cannot see or access it. What we receive when you send feedback or email us is described below.

Public campaign information: The membership page retrieves current Pro campaigns and claim periods over HTTPS from the public campaign configuration on stub.ink. This request does not send ledger data, Apple Account information, feedback IDs or unique device identifiers. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or tracking.

GitHub Pages hosts the campaign configuration and this website. To serve the pages and configuration, the hosting provider GitHub may receive basic network information such as your IP address, request time and requested path. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data stored on your device

Entries, accounts, categories, notes, tags, budgets, recurring plans, templates, refunds and reimbursements, loans, credit-card statements, edit history and app preferences are stored in the app's on-device storage to show your ledger. The app does not automatically upload your ledger. Files you choose to export or share, and screenshots you include with feedback, are covered below.

If you add a Stub Home Screen or Lock Screen widget or the Control Center button, the app writes a summary for the widgets to storage shared between the app and its widgets on your device: today's and this month's income and spending, the last 7 days of spending, the remaining budget, your top 3 spending categories, and upcoming plans (name and amount). It contains no individual entries, notes or account names, stays on your device and is never sent to us. When App Lock or Hide Amounts is on, widgets do not show amounts.

Permissions

Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode is used only if you turn on App Lock; iOS performs the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded. Notifications are requested only if you turn on local reminders. The app does not use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendars or health data.

Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app checks your subscription status on-device through StoreKit; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Export and backups

JSON and CSV exports are not encrypted; you decide where they go. Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data and are handled by Apple or your computer. No-ledger diagnostics are created only when you export them, or when you choose Report a Problem, whether that goes out by email from Settings or by direct submission after a shake. Suggestions do not include them. Direct submission always includes them for a problem report, and the form has no separate switch to remove them; in the email composer you can delete the attachment. They contain technical status such as the app version and storage format, and no amounts, names, notes or device identifiers. You still decide whether an email is sent.

Feedback and offer codes

There are two feedback paths that you choose to use. Feedback in Settings sends email after a separate consent step, and the system composer opens only when Mail can send. Shaking your iPhone opens a form that submits directly from the app after you confirm. Their receiving services and confirmation steps differ.

Direct in-app submission: Shake your iPhone to open the feedback form (you can turn shaking off on the Feedback page). Choose Report a Problem or Suggestion and write your feedback. When you tap Send (发送), the app shows the submission notice; nothing is sent until you tap Agree and Submit (同意并提交). A submission contains:

  • your message and, optionally, contact details you enter (such as an email address) so we can reply;
  • a feedback ID: a random string such as S-7K2M-QX9D generated on your device, not derived from any device identifier, Apple Account or ledger data, and replaced if you reinstall the app;
  • the app version and build, iOS version and model code (for example iPhone16,1, a model, not a device identifier);
  • for a problem report, the no-ledger diagnostics described above; suggestions do not include them. Direct submission has no separate switch to remove the diagnostics;
  • optionally (on by default, and you can turn it off on the feedback page), a screenshot of the current screen. Before it is sent, the app finds numbers using on-device text recognition and covers them; this happens on your iPhone, not over the network. If the numbers cannot be covered, the screenshot is not attached. Account names, category names, notes and other text may still be visible, so you can preview the screenshot and decide whether to include it.

The text you write, any contact details you add and the screenshot are visible on the feedback page. The app version, iOS version, model code, feedback ID and, for a problem report, the diagnostics are included in the submission and described by category in the confirmation notice; the page does not show each prefilled value. Canceling the confirmation sends nothing and does not affect local bookkeeping. Submissions do not automatically include your ledger, backup files or device identifiers. If the network fails, only feedback you have already confirmed is kept on your device and sent when you are back online. At most 5 items stay in that queue; an older unsent item is removed first when the queue is full. Anything still unsent after 7 days is deleted.

Submissions are sent over HTTPS to our server on Alibaba Cloud in mainland China (api.stub.ink), received and stored by Texture Factory (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd., without passing through any third-party email service. Feedback records do not store your IP address. The deployment configuration turns off access logs for this site; to prevent abuse, recent submissions are counted per IP in memory only and that count is not written into the feedback record. Error logs kept for troubleshooting may still be retained. These direct submissions are kept for 365 days after we receive them and are then deleted automatically by that server.

Email feedback: In Settings › About › Feedback (设置 › 关于 › 意见反馈), choose Report a Problem or Suggestion. Before any mail step, the app explains the recipient, purpose and information involved in sending personal information outside mainland China. After you tap Agree and Continue (同意并继续), the system Mail composer opens only if Mail can send; you can edit or remove text and attachments and decide whether to send. If it cannot, the app hands the message to the default mail app, or, if that also fails, only asks you to copy the address and feedback ID and write yourself. Canceling does not start a mail step and does not affect local bookkeeping. You can also email developer@txturefty.com directly.

When you email us, we receive your email address, message and attachments, plus any prefilled app version, iOS version, model code, feedback ID and optional no-ledger diagnostics you leave in the email. Google Workspace handles the mail, which may be stored outside mainland China. Email feedback is kept for at most 12 months from the day we receive it, counted as 365 days, the same maximum as a direct submission. We delete these messages manually; the email service does not purge them automatically. You may ask us to delete them. After we verify your identity, we will do so within 15 working days. Please do not send JSON backups or screenshots with uncovered amounts. Children under 14 should not submit feedback.

We use feedback and email only to reply, fix problems, improve the app, and to send you a Pro offer code as thanks when we adopt your suggestion or confirm a problem you reported. We never use them for advertising, sell them or share them, except where required by law. You can ask us at any time, quoting your feedback ID, to access, correct or delete what you sent.

Offer codes are Apple subscription offer codes, redeemed in the app (Stub 会员 › 兑换码) or in the App Store. Apple handles redemption and the subscription; we do not receive your Apple Account details.

Retention and deletion

Ledger data stays on your device until you delete entries, use "Clear all ledger data" in Settings, or delete the app. Files you exported and system backups are not affected. Because we never hold your ledger data, we cannot restore it for you. Direct submissions are deleted automatically by our server 365 days after receipt. Email feedback is kept for at most 12 months and is deleted by us manually; the email service does not purge it automatically. To access, correct or delete feedback or emails you sent us, contact us and include your feedback ID for in-app feedback. After we verify your identity, we will process that request within 15 working days.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14. A child should read this policy with a parent or other guardian before using the app.

Changes and contact

We will post any update on this page. If we add further online or syncing features, or new features that process personal data, we will explain them before they are enabled and obtain consent where required by law. Contact: developer@txturefty.com. We reply within 15 working days.
