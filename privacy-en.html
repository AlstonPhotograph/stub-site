Privacy Policy

Stub is a bookkeeping app that stores your ledger locally on your iPhone. There is no account, ledger upload or bank connection. The membership page retrieves public campaign information over the internet; we receive information from you only when you choose to submit feedback or email us.

Operator: Texture Factory (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd. · Effective: October 9, 2026 · Updated: November 8, 2026

Data we collect

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking code. Everything you enter in your ledger stays on your device, and we cannot see or access it. What we receive when you send feedback or email us is described below.

Public campaign information: The membership page retrieves current Pro campaigns and claim periods over HTTPS from the public campaign configuration on stub.ink. This request does not send ledger data, Apple Account information, feedback IDs or unique device identifiers. We do not use it for advertising, analytics or tracking.

GitHub Pages hosts the campaign configuration and this website. To serve the pages and configuration, the hosting provider GitHub may receive basic network information such as your IP address, request time and requested path. GitHub Pages logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Data stored on your device

Entries, accounts, categories, notes, tags, budgets, recurring plans, templates, refunds and reimbursements, loans, credit-card statements, edit history and app preferences are stored in the app's on-device storage and used only to show your ledger.

If you add a Stub Home Screen or Lock Screen widget or the Control Center button, the app writes a summary for the widgets to storage shared between the app and its widgets on your device: today's and this month's income and spending, the last 7 days of spending, the remaining budget, your top 3 spending categories, and upcoming plans (name and amount). It contains no individual entries, notes or account names, stays on your device and is never sent to us. When App Lock or Hide Amounts is on, widgets do not show amounts.

Permissions

Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode is used only if you turn on App Lock; iOS performs the check and the app only learns whether it succeeded. Notifications are requested only if you turn on local reminders. The app does not use the camera, photos, microphone, location, contacts, calendars or health data.

Subscriptions

Pro subscriptions are sold and charged by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. The app checks your subscription status on-device through StoreKit; we never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details.

Export and backups

JSON and CSV exports are not encrypted; you decide where they go. Device backups (iCloud or computer) may include the app's data and are handled by Apple or your computer. Diagnostics are created only when you export them or include them with a problem report, and contain no amounts, names, notes or device identifiers.

Feedback and offer codes

Open the feedback page from Settings › About › Feedback (设置 › 关于 › 意见反馈), or by shaking your iPhone on any screen (you can turn shaking off on the Feedback page). Choose Report a Problem or Suggestion and write your feedback. When you tap Submit, the app shows what will be sent; nothing is sent until you tap Agree and Submit. A submission contains:

  • your message and, optionally, contact details you enter (such as an email address) so we can reply;
  • a feedback ID: a random string such as S-7K2M-QX9D generated on your device, not derived from any device identifier, Apple Account or ledger data, and replaced if you reinstall the app;
  • the app version and build, iOS version and model code (for example iPhone16,1, a model, not a device identifier);
  • for problem reports, optionally the no-ledger diagnostics described above;
  • optionally (on by default, can be turned off on the feedback page), a screenshot of the current screen. Before it is sent, the app finds numbers using on-device text recognition and covers them; this happens on your iPhone, not over the network. Account names, category names, notes and other text may still be visible, so you can preview the screenshot and decide whether to include it.

Submissions do not include your ledger, backup files or device identifiers. If you are offline, the submission is kept on your device and sent automatically when you are back online; anything not sent within 7 days is deleted.

Submissions are sent over HTTPS to our server on Alibaba Cloud in mainland China (api.stub.ink), received and stored by Texture Factory (Hangzhou) Technology Co., Ltd., without passing through any third-party email service. The server does not store your IP address or keep access logs; to prevent abuse it counts recent submissions per IP in memory only. Submissions are deleted automatically 12 months after we receive them.

You can also email developer@txturefty.com. When you email us we receive your email address, your message and anything you attach. Mail is stored with our email provider (Google Workspace), whose servers may be outside mainland China, and kept for up to 2 years after the issue is closed. Please do not send JSON backups or screenshots with uncovered amounts.

We use feedback and email only to reply, fix problems, improve the app, and to send you a Pro offer code as thanks when we adopt your suggestion or confirm a problem you reported. We never use them for advertising, sell them or share them, except where required by law. You can ask us at any time, quoting your feedback ID, to access, correct or delete what you sent.

Offer codes are Apple subscription offer codes, redeemed in the app (Stub 会员 › 兑换码) or in the App Store. Apple handles redemption and the subscription; we do not receive your Apple Account details.

Retention and deletion

Ledger data stays on your device until you delete entries, use "Clear all ledger data" in Settings, or delete the app. Files you exported and system backups are not affected. Because we never hold your ledger data, we cannot restore it for you. To access, correct or delete feedback or emails you sent us, contact us (include your feedback ID for in-app feedback).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 14.

Changes and contact

We will post any update on this page. If we add further online or syncing features, or new features that process personal data, we will explain them before they are enabled and obtain consent where required by law. Contact: developer@txturefty.com.
